# 📊 Financial Performance Analysis & Interactive Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business financial performance using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective was to transform raw financial data into an interactive dashboard that tracks revenue, profit, profit margins, discounts, and sales performance across business segments, countries, and time periods.

The project combines data transformation, financial analysis, interactive visualization, and business insight generation to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue, profit, and profitability.
* Compare financial performance across business segments and countries.
* Identify monthly and yearly revenue and profit trends.
* Evaluate the relationship between discounting and profitability.
* Build interactive dashboards with dynamic slicers.
* Communicate key business findings through clear visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
* **DAX** – Measures and financial KPI calculations
* **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPI cards, and financial analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Financial Performance Dashboard

* KPI cards for Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Units Sold, and Average Discount.
* Profit analysis by segment and country.
* Revenue and profit margin trends.
* Interactive filtering for dynamic financial analysis.

### 2. Segment & Country Analysis

* Segment-wise profit, sales volume, and discount analysis.
* Country-wise revenue and profit comparisons.
* Ribbon chart to analyze changes in segment profit rankings over time.

### 3. Financial Trends & Performance

* Monthly revenue and profit margin trends.
* Yearly revenue vs. profit comparison.
* Scatter chart to explore the relationship between discount percentage and profit margin.
* Waterfall chart to visualize the financial bridge from revenue to profit through discounts and costs.

## 🔍 Key Business Observations

The dashboard enables the identification and investigation of the following business patterns:

* **Profitability Differences:** Financial contributions vary across segments and countries, helping identify key profit-generating areas.
* **Revenue vs. Profit:** Higher revenue does not necessarily translate into proportionately higher profit.
* **Changing Segment Rankings:** The ribbon chart highlights shifts in relative segment profitability over time.
* **Discount–Profitability Relationship:** The scatter chart helps assess whether higher discounting is associated with lower profit margins.
* **Revenue-to-Profit Conversion:** The waterfall chart highlights how discounts and costs contribute to the difference between gross revenue and final profit.

*These observations describe the analytical capabilities of the dashboard; specific conclusions depend on the selected filters and underlying data.*
